How to Study Effectively

Best study tips for students to focus, manage time and score better in exams.

1. Make a Study Timetable

A study timetable helps students manage time and complete subjects on time. You should study at the same time every day to build a habit. Use our Timetable Maker to create your timetable.

2. Use Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes and take a 5 minute break. This method improves focus. Use our Pomodoro Timer for study sessions.

3. Track Your Study Time

Tracking study time helps you stay disciplined and productive. Use our Study Timer to track study hours.

4. Set Exam Countdown

When you know how many days are left for exam, you study more seriously. Use our Exam Countdown Timer.

5. Track Daily Tasks

Write daily study tasks and complete them one by one. Use our Task Tracker.

Best Study Tips

• Remove distractions like mobile phone
• Study in a quiet place
• Make short notes
• Revise daily
• Sleep 7-8 hours
• Drink water and stay healthy
