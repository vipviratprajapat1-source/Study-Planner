How to Focus on Study

1. Remove Distractions

Keep your phone away while studying. Study in a quiet place.

2. Use Pomodoro Timer

Study for 25 minutes and take a 5 minute break.

3. Make Study Plan

Write what you will study before starting.

4. Keep Study Table Clean

A clean table helps you focus better.

5. Sleep Properly

Sleep 7–8 hours daily for better concentration.
